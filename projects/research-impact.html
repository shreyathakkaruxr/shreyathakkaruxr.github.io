Research impact tracking

A process for recording what teams do with research findings after a study ends.

Follow-up

Send a follow-up 4–6 weeks after each study.

Send four questions to the stakeholders who used the study.

ResearcherSends the follow-up
  1. What changed after the study?
  2. Which metric are you tracking?
  3. Who else should I contact?
  4. Can I share your quote?
StakeholdersPM, design, and engineering leads

Weekly log

Update the log every Friday.

Spend 10 minutes each week recording new evidence.

Every Friday · 10 minFollow up 4–6 weeks after each study

  1. Plan

    Set the decision, metric, and baseline at kickoff.

  2. Signals

    Record who attended and where findings were cited.

  3. Impact log

    Record who used the research, what they did, and what changed.

  4. Confirmation

    Ask stakeholders to confirm what changed and why.

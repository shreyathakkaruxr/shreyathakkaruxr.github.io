Research Impact Mapping

What happens to research after the study?

Overview

How a study’s findings reach teams, shape decisions, change products, and move metrics.

Why map impact?

Research pays off when a team acts on it, often weeks later and out of sight. Mapping links each study to the decisions it shaped.

  • Proves valueShows the return on research when budgets tighten.
  • Captures invisible winsFeatures cut, risks avoided, new ways of working.
  • Brings research upstreamTeams that see research shape decisions involve it from the start.

Context

Evidence of research impact lives in chats, roadmaps and memory, so quarterly reports listed the number of studies completed.

Constraints

  • TimeHad to fit into a weekly routine.
  • Long cyclesHardware outcomes can arrive months after a study.
  • AccessProduct and business data is scattered across teams and tools.

How I approached it

I adapted an industry framework for a hardware org with three changes:

Inspired by Nikki Anderson, The User Research Strategist.

  1. Name the decision up frontEach study brief states the decision it informs.
  2. Log early impactTrack signals and decisions as leading indicators.
  3. Keep time and effort lowTen minutes a week to log, one follow-up at 4–6 weeks, and a one-page report each quarter.

In practice

Four steps to record impact after each study.

1

Send a follow-up

4–6 weeks after each study, to the PM, design, and engineering leads

  1. What changed after the study?
  2. Which metric are you tracking?
  3. Who else should I contact?
  4. Can I share your quote?

2

Update the log

Every Friday · 10 min

  1. PlanSet the decision, metric, and baseline at kickoff.
  2. SignalsRecord who attended and where findings were cited.
  3. Impact logRecord who used the research, what they did, and what changed.
  4. ConfirmationAsk stakeholders to confirm what changed and why.

3

Tag each entry by horizon

From early signals to measured outcomes

    4

    Build the report from the log

    Confirmed entries, grouped by horizon

    Q3 report

    Headline
    Top stories
    Decisions shaped
    • Add a quarter summary and next-quarter focus areas.
    • Share quotes only with permission.

    ↻ Repeat for each study

    Impact horizons

    Each log entry gets one horizon.

    Signal

    Teams are engaging with the research

    Evidence
    Sessions joined. Stakeholders attended sessions, readouts, or field visits
    Findings quoted. Findings appear in decks, specs, roadmaps, or leadership updates
    When it shows up
    During the study and in the first weeks after

    Decision

    The research informed a decision

    Evidence
    Decision confirmed. A stakeholder confirms the study informed a specific decision
    Risk avoided. A feature, launch, or investment was changed, paused, or stopped
    When it shows up
    Within weeks to a few months

    Change

    A product or process changed

    Evidence
    Product change. A feature, flow, hardware, or content change
    Ways of working. A team adopted new criteria, rituals, or methods
    When it shows up
    Within one to two quarters

    Outcome

    A metric moved against the baseline

    Evidence
    Metric moved. The kickoff metric changed, such as satisfaction, task success, returns, or support calls
    Cost or time saved. Rework, support load, or development time decreased
    When it shows up
    After launch, often two or more quarters later

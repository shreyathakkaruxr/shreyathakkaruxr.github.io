Laundry Systems

The next generation of laundry systems, in 16 weeks.

Overview

End-to-end research for the next generation of washers, dryers, and app: 35+ studies in 16 weeks, from discovery to design freeze.

Role
Lead UX Researcher, Human Factors
Timeline
2026 (16 weeks to design freeze)
Teams
Industrial Design, CMF, Product Management, Hardware Engineering, Global UX

Context

Washers and dryers are already connected, controlled from the machine and the phone. This work shaped the next generation of connected consumer tech, across washer, dryer, tower, and app interaction, following the global brand visual design identity.

Opportunities

  • Connected routinesWi-Fi connectivity, delayed start, auto-dose, and app notifications.
  • Accessible controlsKnobs and readable embedded screens across tiered products, for all ages and abilities.
  • Favorite cyclesSave go-to cycles, with energy and water data for each.

Constraints

  • Compressed timeline16 weeks to design freeze, down from 32, with hardware decisions locked early.
  • Global marketsNeeds and expectations vary by region.

How I approached it

Three moves kept research ahead of hardware decisions:

  1. Define premium earlyPerception and futures research defined signals of premium.
  2. Test with physical mockupsHuman factors and RITE rounds removed use errors early.
  3. Design for error recovery and notificationsMapped all error scenarios, with clear alerts and recovery steps.

Research roadmap

35+ studies, from discovery to design freeze.

1

Discover

5 studies · homes → needs

  • Contextual inquiryIn-home visits and interviews, NA · EU · LATAM · Asia
  • Needs findingLatent needs, opportunities, and pain points
  • Competitive benchmarkingSide by side against competitors

2

Define

5 studies · needs → brief

  • Perception and futures researchWhat signals premium, now and next
  • Design briefPremium triggers, priorities, and opportunities for hardware, UI, and connectivity

3

Develop

15+ studies · brief → concepts

  • Concept testingFront-load, top-load, and tower, with ID, design identity, and CMF
  • CMF researchMaterials and finishes, conscious and subconscious response
  • Icon comprehensionControl panel and app
  • Human factors testingReach, knob force, door handling, and display legibility, with physical mockups

4

Deliver

10 studies · concepts → design freeze

  • Embodied UI testingControl panel tasks on washer and dryer
  • App interaction testingRemote start, settings, and notifications
  • Usability studiesRITE rounds on onboarding, core tasks, and error recovery
  • Design validationIn-person studies across 3 cities
  • Quant researchLarge-scale online validation, n = 500+, 2 markets

Impact to date

The next generation reached design freeze in 16 weeks.

  • $50MExpansion validated
  • ~60%Reduction in task time
  • 40%Faster concept-to-market
  • 1Interaction language across washers, dryers, and app

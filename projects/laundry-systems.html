Laundry Systems

The next generation of laundry systems, in 16 weeks.

Overview

End-to-end research for the next generation of washers, dryers, and app: 35+ studies in 16 weeks, from discovery to design freeze.

Role
Lead UX Researcher, Human Factors
Timeline
2026 (16 weeks to design freeze)
Teams
Industrial Design, CMF, Product Management, Hardware Engineering, Global UX

Context

Washers and dryers are already connected, controlled from the machine and the phone. This work shaped the next generation of connected consumer tech, across washer, dryer, tower, and app interaction, following the global brand visual design identity.

Opportunities

  • Connected routinesWi-Fi connectivity, delayed start, auto-dose, and app notifications.
  • Accessible controlsKnobs and readable embedded screens across tiered products, for all ages and abilities.
  • Favorite cyclesSave go-to cycles, with energy and water data for each.

Constraints

  • Compressed timeline16 weeks to design freeze, down from 32, with hardware decisions locked early.
  • Global marketsNeeds and expectations vary by region.

How I approached it

Three moves kept research ahead of hardware decisions:

  1. Define premium earlyPerception and futures research defined signals of premium.
  2. Test with physical mockupsHuman factors and RITE rounds caught usability gaps early.
  3. Design for error recovery and notificationsMapped all error scenarios, with clear alerts and recovery steps.

Develop

  • Quant researchLarge-scale online validation, n = 500+, 2 markets

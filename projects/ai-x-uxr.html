UX research practice

AI x UXR

How can research teams use AI to speed up insights and keep their rigor?

Overview

A shared practice where AI drafts and researchers decide.

Role
Lead UX Researcher, Research Practice
Timeline
2025–present
Teams
UX Research across four business units, Global UX

Context

AI tools spread across the research team, and each researcher used them differently. Without shared methods, analysis quality depended on the researcher.

This project built a shared practice for AI in research: faster analysis, with methods stakeholders can trust.

Constraints

  • Tools6+ tools, with no central repository.
  • Time and resourcesBuilt alongside active research projects, without dedicated time.
  • Pace of changeAI tools changed every few months, so the practice had to outlast any single tool.

How I approached it

Five decisions shaped the practice:

  1. Focus AI on repetitive tasksInterpretation and cultural analysis stay human-led.
  2. Engineer the contextShared templates (context, role, data, format) keep outputs consistent.
  3. Keep templates livingPrompts and playbooks are reviewed each quarter as tools and skills change.
  4. Disclose AI useEach report notes AI’s role, so stakeholders can trust the findings.
  5. Reinvest time in storytellingTime saved goes into synthesis and storytelling.
